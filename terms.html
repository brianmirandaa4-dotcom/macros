Syncope terms of use

Last updated November 6, 2026. Draft pending legal review.

Not medical advice. Syncope gives general nutrition, hydration and training information for healthy adults. It does not diagnose, treat or prevent any condition. Talk to a doctor or registered dietitian before changing your diet or training, especially if you are pregnant, have a medical condition, take medication, or have a history of disordered eating. Stop and seek help if anything feels wrong.

Who can use Syncope

You must be at least 13 years old. If you are under 18, use Syncope with a parent or guardian's permission. Syncope never sets a calorie deficit for anyone under 18.

Estimates, not guarantees

Calorie, macro, hydration and training numbers are estimates based on published research and the information you give. Photo scans, label scans and wearable data can be wrong. Check anything important yourself.

Your account and data

You are responsible for what you log and share. Our privacy policy explains what we store and how to delete it.

Wearables and other services

WHOOP, Garmin, Oura and Apple Health are separate services with their own terms. Syncope is not made, sponsored or endorsed by them. Their names and logos belong to their owners and are used only to show where your data came from.

Friends and fair play

Don't harass other users, impersonate anyone, or share someone else's information. We may remove profiles that break these rules.

Changes and availability

Syncope is provided as is. Features may change or stop. We will post updated terms here.
