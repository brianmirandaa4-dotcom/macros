Syncope privacy policy

Last updated November 4, 2026.

What Syncope stores

Everything you log in Syncope (food, workouts, weigh-ins, check-ins, your plan and profile) is saved only in your browser on your own device. It is not uploaded to us.

WHOOP and Garmin data

If you choose to connect WHOOP or Garmin, a small sync server operated by the Syncope owner on Cloudflare stores the sign-in tokens needed to read your data (recovery, sleep, heart rate, strain, workouts, steps and similar metrics) and a short-lived copy of the last few weeks of that data, so Syncope can show it to you. This data is only sent back to your own copy of Syncope. It is never sold, shared, or used for advertising.

Disconnecting

Use Plan → Connections → Disconnect in Syncope to remove your tokens and cached data from the sync server. You can also revoke access from your WHOOP or Garmin account settings at any time.
