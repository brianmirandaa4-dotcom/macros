Trainer privacy policy

Last updated November 6, 2026 (teams and eating rules added).

The short version
  • Your food log, workouts, weigh-ins and plan live on your phone, and are backed up to your account if you create one.
  • We never sell your data, never use it for advertising, and never share it with data brokers.
  • Wearable data (WHOOP, Garmin, Oura, Apple Health) is only used to show you your own health and fitness information, and is never sent to an AI model.
  • Friends only see what you choose to share, and you can stop sharing at any time.

What stays on your device

Everything you log in Trainer (food, drinks, water, workouts, weigh-ins, check-ins, body measurements, your plan and profile) is saved in your browser on your own device. It is not uploaded to us unless you turn on one of the features below.

Your account and backups

If you create an account, our server stores your username, a salted and hashed version of your password (never the password itself), and an automatic backup of your log so you can sign in and get your data back on any phone. The backup is only readable by your signed-in account. You can ask us to delete your account and backups at any time.

Wearables: WHOOP, Garmin and Oura

If you connect a wearable, you sign in with that company and approve access yourself. Our sync server (run on Cloudflare) stores the access tokens and a short-lived copy of recent data (such as recovery, sleep, heart rate, HRV, strain, steps and workouts) so Trainer can show it to you. That data is only sent back to your own copy of Trainer. It is not shared with other companies, not used to train or prompt any AI model, and not used for advertising.

Apple Health

If you import or connect Apple Health data, it is used only for health and fitness features inside Trainer. It is never used for advertising, never sold, and never shared with a third party without your express permission.

Photo and label scanning

When you scan a meal, label, barcode or drink with the camera, or ask about a food by name, that photo or text is sent to Anthropic's Claude service (directly, or through our sync server) to estimate nutrition. Only the food photo or text is sent, not your wearable or health data. We do not keep the photo. Barcode and brand searches are looked up in the public Open Food Facts database. Voice input uses your device or browser's own speech recognition (which Apple or Google may process); only the resulting text is used, exactly like typed text. Restaurant lookups send the text you typed to Claude, which may search the web for that restaurant's public menu.

Trainer Friends

If you join Trainer Friends, our server stores your username, profile picture, an optional one-way hashed phone number (so friends can find you), and the daily summaries you choose to share (for example calories, macros, water, workouts and recovery). Use Friends → Privacy to choose what is shared. Use "Leave Trainer Friends" to delete your social profile.

Teams

If you join a team with your coach's code, our server keeps a copy of your last two weeks of food log (foods, amounts, meals, times, calories and macros), your meal check-ins and your weigh-ins (morning, pre-practice and post-practice), so your team's coaches can see them. Coaches never see your wearable data (WHOOP, Garmin, Oura or Apple Health), sleep, recovery, or your coach-chat history. Teams are for people 18 and older, and you agree to this sharing when you join. Leave the team at any time in Trainer and the shared copy is deleted right away. A coach can remove a player, which also deletes that copy.

Eating rules and food traditions

If you tell Trainer how you eat (for example halal, kosher, vegetarian or no pork) or which food traditions you like, that choice stays on your device and in your account backup. When Trainer asks AI for meal ideas, it sends only the food rules themselves (for example "no pork, no alcohol"), never your religion.

Notifications

If you turn on rest-timer notifications, our server stores a push subscription so it can send you a "rest is over" alert. It contains no health data.

Children

Trainer is not meant for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them. Trainer never sets a calorie deficit for anyone under 18.

Security and breaches

Data on our server is transmitted over HTTPS and access tokens are never shown to other users. If a breach ever exposes your information, we will notify you, and regulators where required, without unreasonable delay.

Your choices

  • Disconnect a wearable in Trainer (Profile → Wearables) to delete its tokens and cached data from our server. You can also revoke access in your WHOOP, Garmin, Oura or Apple Health settings.
  • Export or delete your log at any time in Profile → Backup & export.
  • Ask us to delete anything we hold about you by emailing the address below.

Not medical advice

Trainer gives general nutrition and fitness information. See our terms.
